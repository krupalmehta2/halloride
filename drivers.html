For drivers

Have an empty seat? Share the journey.

If supported by your current Hallo app, list your planned journey and available seats to connect with passengers travelling in the same direction.

Explore the current app

Driver listing features, route availability, and any arrangements are determined by the current Hallo app. Review the app before planning a trip around a listing.

This page does not promise that listing a seat, finding a passenger, cost sharing, or a particular route is available. Verify the feature and terms in the app.

Considering sharing a ride?

  • Confirm that the app currently supports offering a seat.
  • Check trip details and communicate any arrangements using supported options.
  • Follow applicable road rules and the app's current terms.
