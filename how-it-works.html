How it works

A shared journey, in a few simple steps.

These are general steps for exploring a shared ride. Exact workflows, features, routes, and availability depend on the current Hallo app.

Choose your journey

Explore rides that match your route and travel plans, if available for your journey in the app.

Find or offer a seat

Passengers can explore available seats. Drivers can list a journey if that option is supported in the current app.

Share the journey

Check the details in the app and coordinate any arrangements directly as permitted by its current features.

Before you travel: Confirm route and trip details in the app. Do not assume a ride is available or booked until the app explicitly confirms it. Payment, cancellation, messaging, verification, and safety features have not been independently confirmed here.

For passengers

Start by exploring available journeys that fit your plans. Review the current app information carefully before deciding whether a ride works for you.

For drivers

If your Hallo app supports offering seats, follow the in-app process to share your planned journey. Do not rely on this page as confirmation that a particular driver feature is available.
