Created in Gujarat. Made for shared journeys in India.

Hallo is an Indian ride-sharing app created in Gujarat. We believe journeys can feel more connected when people travelling in the same direction have a way to find one another.

Ride availability depends on current listings and supported locations; a match or ride is not guaranteed. Check the app for the latest options in your area.
