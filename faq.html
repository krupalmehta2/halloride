Frequently asked questions

Answers for your next shared journey.

These answers describe the website and known app availability. For current routes and in-app features, refer to Hallo on Google Play or contact support.

What is Hallo?

Hallo is a ride-sharing and carpooling app for discovering shared journeys. Check its current Google Play listing for verified, up-to-date app details.

How does ride sharing work?

People travelling in the same direction may be able to find a ride or offer an available seat. See How It Works. Exact steps and features depend on the current app.

How can I find a shared ride?

Explore available journeys in the Android app. Ride availability and supported routes may vary, and no ride is guaranteed.

Can I offer an available seat?

The app may support listing a planned journey and available seats. Please check the current app to confirm whether driver listings are available to you.

Is Hallo available on Android?

Yes. The Hallo Android app is available on Google Play: open the official listing.

Is an iOS version available?

iOS is coming soon. There is no App Store download link at this time.

How can I contact support?

Email hallorideapp@gmail.com or use the contact page.

Are all Gujarat cities and routes available?

This website does not claim that every location or route is operating. Check the current app for available journeys.

How do payments, bookings, or cancellations work?

These details have not been independently confirmed for this website. Please review the current app and its terms before making arrangements.
