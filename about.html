About Hallo

Shared journeys start with a common direction.

Hallo is a ride-sharing and carpooling app for people looking to discover journeys they can share. The idea is simple: when travel plans point the same way, it may be possible to make more of the trip together.

Making shared travel easier to discover

Finding a shared ride can begin with finding other people whose route and plans are similar to yours. Hallo aims to make those shared journeys easier to discover, whether you are looking for a ride or have a seat to offer through options supported in the current app.

Hallo is not presented here as a taxi operator, and a ride, route, seat, or match is not guaranteed. Please check the current app listing and app experience for available services and details.
